01 / The Problem

One in five patients never shows up.

Across 110,527 appointments at clinics in Vitória, Brazil, 20.2% of patients simply did not appear — costing clinics time, resources, and lost care for other patients who needed those slots.

That's over 22,000 missed appointments in a single dataset — and millions more across healthcare systems worldwide.

02 / Age

Youth skips. Age shows up.

Patients in their 20s and 30s miss appointments at the highest rates — up to 27%. The trend reverses sharply after 50, where patients are far more consistent attendees.

Infants and toddlers (under 10) also show surprisingly high no-show rates, likely reflecting parent scheduling pressures.

Patients aged 20–29 are 40% more likely to miss than patients aged 60–69.

03 / Wait Time

The longer you wait, the less you show.

Same-day appointments have a near-zero no-show rate. But as wait time grows, so does the chance of absence. Appointments scheduled 30+ days out see no-show rates spike above 35%.

Reducing wait times from 30 days to 7 days could cut no-shows nearly in half.

04 / SMS Reminders

The SMS paradox.

Counter-intuitively, patients who received an SMS reminder showed up less often than those who didn't. This is a confounding effect — SMS reminders were disproportionately sent to patients with longer wait times.

Raw data suggests SMS = worse outcomes. Reality: timing matters more than reminders.

05 / Health Conditions

Chronic illness drives commitment.

Patients with hypertension or diabetes consistently show lower no-show rates. Patients with alcoholism have the highest no-show rate of any condition group.

Health literacy and felt urgency are powerful predictors of attendance.

Key Takeaways

What the Data Tells Us

Five actionable insights for healthcare administrators to reduce missed appointments.

Shorten Wait Times

Wait time is the strongest predictor of no-show. Appointments >30 days away face 3× higher absence rates than same-week bookings.

Target Young Adults

Patients aged 18–35 are the highest-risk group. Tailored interventions — app-based reminders, flexible rescheduling — could significantly reduce their absence rate.

Fix the SMS System

Reminders are being sent too late — after long wait gaps are already in place. Smarter, earlier, and more personalized communication is needed.

Focus on High-Risk Areas

A handful of neighbourhoods account for a disproportionate share of no-shows. Geographic targeting can maximize intervention ROI.

Leverage Chronic Care

Patients with chronic illness show up more reliably. Healthcare systems should build on this engaged patient base while addressing psychosocial barriers for others.
